# ProxyChoiceApp — your proxy, your choice of apps

Need a proxy for your browser or Microsoft Store while your other apps keep using their regular connection? **ProxyChoiceApp** puts application routing in one clear, easy-to-use window.

Enter your server, port, username and password, choose the apps, and connect. Select Chrome and Edge independently. Microsoft Store is enabled by default, and an installed YouTube app is added when detected. YouTube running in a browser follows that browser's route.

**Built for everyday convenience:**

- Save multiple proxies with SOCKS5, HTTP and HTTPS support.
- Check your proxy IP and view current speed, received data and sent data.
- Disconnect when you need to, with a red or green status icon.
- A compact window, readable text and detailed built-in help.
- Six interface languages with flags, plus a clearable, exportable journal.
- Encrypted storage for saved settings and passwords in Windows.

Run the standalone **EXE** or use the ready-made installer for Windows 10/11 x64. All required components are included.

**Choose your apps. Control your connection. Make proxy routing easier with ProxyChoiceApp.**
